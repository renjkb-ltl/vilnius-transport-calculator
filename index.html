How to use this calculator
1
Enter your car details — purchase price, fuel type, loan, insurance, and parking. The calculator fills in reasonable estimates so you only need to override what you know.
2
Set the Alternatives section — configure how you would actually move around without a car: public transport pass, carsharing trips, occasional taxi. Set it to match your real lifestyle, not a perfect scenario.
3
Read the results — all four options appear side by side with the true monthly cost, including the hidden costs most people miss (depreciation, tyres, accident risk). The Car-free mix card shows what switching would actually cost.
All figures are estimates based on Vilnius market data. Override any field with your actual numbers for a more accurate picture.
